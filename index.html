Format reference

One entry per line; columns line up automatically. FROM/TO and every timecode are S+F (seconds+frames, 24 f/s) relative to this page — 0+00 = frame 1.

Dialogue
FROM   | TO   | SPEAKER   | TEXT
0+00   | 0+12 | ALICE (SE)| (gasp)
2+12   | 4+00 | BOB (M)   | The door is open.

Optional (SE) (M) (OFF) (ME) (N) (T) (ON) (背) (ノンモン) (独) tag → drawn red above the speaker.

Camera
FROM | TO | TYPE | NAME | A | B
0+00 | 0+12 | PAN | PAN | A | B
0+12 | 2+00 | QTU | Zoom to CU | A | B | 0+12
0+00 | 3+00 | Follow | Follow | A | B | C:1+12 | @OL

Trailing: bare S+F = ア (QTU/QTB animation start), LABEL:S+F = middle keyframe, @TYPE = next segment's interpolation. NAME defaults to TYPE.
